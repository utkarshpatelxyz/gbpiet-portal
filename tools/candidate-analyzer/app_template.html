How the mapping works. Reference proficiencies come from the Department 133 Master Skills Expectations (PCI 0–4 technical · JCS 1–7 behavioral · qualitative role competencies) across the seven career levels L1 Trainee → L7 Leader. Career levels and their experience bands come from the TR India Designations Framework and the Expert Itinerary Category Manual. Skill evidence is inferred from CV keywords and seniority signals — always confirm during the interview using the generated question bank. All five source documents are treated as confidential and never transmitted.
